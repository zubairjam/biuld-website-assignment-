What are the problems in construction?

The usual ones are delays, budget overruns and unclear plans. We agree the scope, the schedule and a fixed price before work starts, and report progress every week.

What is best practice in construction?

Plan thoroughly, use certified materials, keep the site safe and keep the client informed. Every Buildream project has one project manager who is responsible for all four.

How long does a typical project take?

A small renovation takes two to four weeks, a home addition one to two months and a full home build around six months. We give you a written timeline with the quote.
